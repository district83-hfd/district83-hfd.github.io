What This Dashboard Does

  • Live – D83 calls, refreshed every 60 sec; filter by station or type
  • City fire ticker – citywide structure fires with 3+ heavy apparatus (engines, ladders, MPE/MPL); Working fire = box 50+ min, District Chief + full box 25+ min, structure box with 2+ units 60+ min, or a Rehab/Cascade unit
  • Navigate – Apple or Google Maps from the station or your location
  • Hydrants – nearest hydrants and distance on fire calls
  • By Shift – every call for a tour, 06:30 to 06:30
  • Unit Run Counts – runs, hours, busiest and out-of-area runs; This Week runs Mon 06:30 to Mon 06:30 and resets every Monday at shift change
  • Working Fires – past working fires with a District 83 unit
  • Transport – D83 units at hospitals now and where they go
  • Heat Map & Metrics – hot spots, busy hours and days, top 10 locations
  • Each year – counts reset Jan 1; past years in the year menu
  • History tabs – update within a few minutes; a notice shows if late

Public City feed: may lag or miss calls. Not official dispatch.

ⓘ Details

Source: City of Houston active incidents feed (public). Calls usually show up in the feed 5–10 minutes after call time. The Live tab reads the feed directly every 60 s. History tabs come from a logger that checks the feed every minute and publishes within a few minutes of a change (at least every 10 minutes); the feed has no history, so counts only cover what the logger saw. Unit times are approximate: they come from when a unit appeared and disappeared in the feed. A few units the feed dropped before they were logged are added from crew reports (counted as runs, with no time).

District 83 units: E, L, T, TW, SQ, R, RS, RE, A, M and AS units of Stations 10, 69, 76 and 83, plus D83 (District 83 chief). Other units with those numbers (safety officers, investigators, other chiefs, etc.) are not counted.

Working fire: a fire box (structure-fire type or a 111/211–811 code) on CAD 50+ minutes from call time, any fire call where a District Chief (D or DC unit) and the first-dispatched box (3+ fire apparatus; at most one released; ambulances not required) are still on CAD 25 minutes after the call and 25 minutes after it first appeared in the City feed (so a late-listed call can’t qualify on feed lag), a structure-fire box with 2+ units on it at the same time for more than 60 minutes, or a Rehab (RH) or Cascade (CC) unit on a fire call. An alarm upgrade (211–811) alone does not make a working fire. When dispatch edits a call’s time, the logger keeps it as one call.

Out of area: a fire unit (E, L, T, TW, SQ, R, RS, RE) on a call in another station’s first-due area, or D83 outside District 83, from the City’s HFD box map (first-due station of the box the call’s point falls in). Outside D83: the call’s first-due station is in another HFD district (districts from the HFD Fire Stations page). Only District 83 units are counted. Calls within 150 m of another station’s area (or another district, for the district count), or with no first-due on the map, are not counted. Ambulances and EMS supervisors are not counted. The City map may not show recent box changes.

Hydrants: nearest hydrants show on fire, smoke, alarm, burn and explosion calls, extra-alarm codes, and any call with an alarm level. City of Houston GIS “Houston Fire Hydrant” layer (in-service hydrants only); distances are straight-line from the City’s geocoded point (block or intersection, not the exact address). Verify on arrival. Not an official dispatch or records tool.

ⓘ About This Data

Run = one unit on one call (3 District 83 units on one call = 3 runs). Incident = one call with at least one District 83 unit on it (those 3 units = 1 incident), so the District 83 Totals card shows both. Incidents are also counted on By Shift and Metrics.

This Week = Monday 06:30 to Monday 06:30 CT; each day is one tour, 06:30 to 06:30 the next morning, with the shift that worked it. Month and Year to Date go by call time (CT).

Busiest = the station’s unit with the most runs in the period (tie: most hours). District Chiefs (D, DC) and EMS Supervisors (AS) are not ranked.

Heavy = the station’s busiest engine or ladder in the period, with MPE runs counted toward its engine and MPL runs toward its ladder; hidden when the station has no engine or ladder runs.

MVI = runs on motor vehicle incidents (crashes, pedestrian and motorcycle calls); vehicle fires count as Fire. Same call types as Metrics.

Hrs = hours committed: the time a unit is listed on each call in the City feed, plus hospital time for ambulances and medic units (A/M), with overlapping time counted once. Shift and district hours add up each unit’s hours. This is the one hours figure used on the dashboard and in the report.

Out of First-Due Area = District 83 fire units (engines, ladders, towers, squads, rescues and D083) on a call in another station’s first-due area (City box map; calls within 150 m of a line are not counted). It follows the period above. The % is that shift’s out-of-area runs out of all its fire-unit runs. Tap a shift to filter and a row to see its runs. Last Wk / Last Mo = the same unit and area in the previous week or month.

MPE/MPL = Manpower Engine and Manpower Ladder, small manpower units a station staffs when its engine or ladder is out of service (e.g. MPE083 runs for Station 83 while E083 is down). They count as District 83 fire units everywhere: their own row under the station, Out of First-Due Area, Call Type Mix, the unit filters and the report. For Station Stripped, MPE0xx stands in for E0xx and MPL0xx for L0xx.

Working fires are listed on the Working Fires tab.

A few units the City feed dropped before they were logged are added from crew reports; they count as runs, with no time.
